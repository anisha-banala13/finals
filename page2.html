TV Show Recommendations

Brooklyn 99

Brooklyn Nine-Nine is a comedic tv series that follows the detectives and staff of the NYPD's fictional 99th Precinct in Brooklyn. The workplace dynamics are shaken up when a highly disciplined, stoic captain takes over and is determined to turn his wildly eccentric and lovable team of detectives into the best in the city.

Cast

  • Andy Samberg, who played Jake Peralta, was on SNL (Saturday Night Live), for the longest time.
  • Andre Braugher, who plays Captain Ray Holt, was in The Wire.
  • Terry Crews, who plays Terry Jeffords, was in Everybody Hates Chris, and White Chicks.
  • Melissa Fumero, who plays Amy Santiago, was in Gossip Girl and One Life to Live.

Gilmore Girls

Gilmore Girls is a beloved comedy-drama that follows the incredibly close bond between single mother Lorelai Gilmore and her daughter, Rory. Set in the tight-knit small town of Stars Hollow, Connecticut, the show explores their daily lives, romances, and the turbulent relationship Lorelai has with her wealthy parents.

Cast

  • Lauren Graham, who plays Lorelai Gilmore, was in Townies and Seinfeld before starring in Gilmore Girls.
  • Alexis Bledel, who plays Rory Gilmore, hadn't acted before Gilmore Girls.
  • Melissa McCarthy, who plays Sookie, was in The Groundlings, which was improv theater, and Go.
  • Scott Patterson, who plays Luke Danes, starred in Seinfeld like Lauren Graham before landing his role in Gilmore Girls.

My Life with the Walter Boys

My Life with the Walter Boys follows Jackie Howard, a driven Manhattan teen whose life is upended when her family dies in a car crash. She relocates to rural Colorado to live with her guardian and mother's best friend, Kathering Walter. There, she must navigate her grief while living on a bustling ranch with ten boys, finding herself caught between two very different brothers: Alex and Cole.

Cast

  • Nikki Rodriguez, who plays Jackie Howard, is best known for playing Vero in On My Block.
  • Noah Lalonde, who plays Cole Walter, appeared as Wes in the thriller movie Deer Camp '86 and guest-starred on Criminal Minds.
  • Ashby Gentry, who plays Alex Walter, is best know for his role on Nickelodeon's Are You Afraid of the Dark?
  • Corey Fogelmanis, who plays Nathan Walter, is known for his role as Farkle Minkus on the Disney Channel series Girl Meets World.
