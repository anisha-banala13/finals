Movie Recommendations

10 Things I Hate About You

10 Things I Hate About You is a 1999 romcom and retelling of Shakespeare's play, The Taming of the Shrew.It follows high school student Cameron, who is in love with his classmate Bianca. Bianca is forbidden from dating until her anti-social older sister Kat finds a boyfriend. To work around this rule, Cameron pays the scariest guy at school, Patrick, to date Kat.

Cast

  • Heath Ledger, who plays Patrick Verona, is famous for his iconic role as the Joker in The Dark Knight, and Ennis Del Mar in Brokeback Mountain
  • Julia Stiles, who plays Kat Stratford, starred in the Jason Bourne action franchise and the TV crime drama Dexter.
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt, who plays Cameron James, is known for leading roles in Inception, 500 Days of Summer, and 3rd Rock from the Sun.
  • Larisa Oleynik, who plays Bianca Stratford, is the childhood star of Nickelodeon's The Secret World of Alex Mack.

Interstellar

Interstellar follows a team of astronauts in a dystopian future who travel through a wormhole near Saturn in search of a new habitable planet as Earth faces extinction from catastrophic crop blight and famine. The main theme of the movie explores the lengths to which humanity will go to survive, entwining familial love and the relativity of time.

Cast

  • Matthew McConaughey, who plays Cooper, is known for his breakout role in Dazed and Confused, his Oscar-winning performance in Dallas Buyers Club, and the critically acclaimed first season of HBO's True Detective.
  • Anne Hathaway, who plays Dr. Amelia Brand, rose to fame in The Princess Diaries, won an Oscar for Les Miserables, starred in Christopher Nolan's The Dark Knight Rises, and led the fashion classic The Devil Wears Prada.
  • Timothee Chalamet, who plays Cooper's son Tom, is also in franchises like Dune, Call Me by Your Name, and Wonka. Interstellar was actually one of his first movies!

Anastasia

Anastasia follows the journey of an amnesiac orphan named Anya who is trying to discover her past. She meets two con artists who are searching fora girl to pass off as the missing Grand Duchess Anastasia to claim a royal reward, completely unaware she is the actual surviving Russian princess.

Cast

  • Meg Ryan, who voices Anastasia, is known as the queen of 1990s romcoms. She's been in When Harry Met Sally..., Sleeples in Seattle, and You've Got Mail.
  • John Cusack, who voices Dimitri, famously held up the boombox in Say Anything..., and starred in Con Air and Grosse Pointe Blank.
  • Kirsten Dunst, who voices Young Anastasia, was just a teenager when she did this film. She became super famous when she starred as Mary Jane Watson in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy, as well as starring roles in Bring It On, Marie Antoinette, and Melancholia.
